Privacy Policy for "The Dash"

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game The Dash (package com.umairgame.thedash), published by [YOUR NAME / STUDIO] ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game has no accounts, chat or login.

Your game progress (best distance, coins, unlocked skins, missions and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Dash is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in your Android settings)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location
  • Device information (model, operating system, language) and app information
  • Ad interactions (for example, ad views and taps) and diagnostic or crash data related to ads

This data is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when required, the game shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad Privacy.
  • Personalized ads: you can opt out on Android in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can also delete your advertising ID.
  • Rewarded ads (for example, "continue" or "double coins") are always optional.

4. Children

The Dash is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help address it.

5. Data security and retention

We do not operate servers that receive your data. Data collected by Google's advertising SDK is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS) and retained according to Google's policies.

6. Permissions used

  • Internet / network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback when you crash (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK for advertising and measurement.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
